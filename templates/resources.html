Cyber-Medical Implants: Pacemakers, Insulin Pumps & More

Devices like pacemakers, insulin pumps, and neurostimulators are already in widespread use and rely on wireless communication for monitoring and updates. Unfortunately, many of these devices have known security vulnerabilities.

In 2017, the FDA recalled 500,000 pacemakers due to cybersecurity risks that could allow unauthorized access to alter pacing or deplete the battery. As these devices become more connected, they become targets for cyberattacks with life-threatening consequences.
